The basics of brewing

Fresh beans, consistent measurements, and a method you enjoy make a noticeable difference.

Choose the right grind

Grind size affects how quickly water extracts flavor. French press usually works best with a coarse grind, while pour-over uses a medium grind. Espresso calls for a fine grind.

Start with a simple ratio

For many filter brewing methods, try 1 gram of coffee for every 16 grams of water. For example, 20 grams of coffee pairs with approximately 320 grams of water. Adjust to taste.

Pay attention to water

For most manual brewing, water just off the boil—roughly 90–96°C (195–205°F)—is a useful starting point. Use fresh, good-tasting water.

Coffee notes

lightbulb Try one change at a time.

If your coffee tastes bitter, try a coarser grind. If it tastes weak or sour, experiment with a finer grind.

Brewing reminder

Measure your coffee and water consistently so you can repeat a recipe you enjoy.
